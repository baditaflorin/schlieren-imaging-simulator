# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project respectful, welcoming, and productive for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and constructive.
- Assume good intent while still being clear about risks and bugs.
- Keep discussion focused on the work.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of project discussion.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block contributors who violate this code of conduct.
